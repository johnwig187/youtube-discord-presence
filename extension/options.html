YouTube → Discord

Start the desktop bridge, then paste its pairing code here. Regular YouTube watch pages and the active mini-player are supported. Refresh an already-open YouTube tab after reloading the extension. Incognito is excluded by default.


Pausing, ads, or closing YouTube clears your activity within about 20 seconds. If several videos play, the most recently started video takes priority. Video title and channel become visible on your Discord profile.
